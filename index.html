How the work goes.

Framing first

Projects rarely come apart during execution. They come apart because the wrong problem got agreed in week one. We spend real time there before anything gets drawn.

Same people throughout

Whoever works out what to build also builds it, so nothing gets lost in a handover between a strategy team and a delivery team.

Small and senior

You get experienced designers in the room rather than a large team with two of them on it.

Long horizons

Our clients plan in years. The work should still hold up by the time they get there.

Quiet by design.

Most of our work sits before launch: early-stage, confidential, not yet ready to be named. We keep our partners' identities private so they can move first.

Questions

Before you write.

What does a project cost?

Framing or diagnostic work runs €5,000 to €8,000. A full product and service project, from strategy to delivery, runs €25,000 to €60,000. Ongoing work on a monthly basis runs €4,000 to €6,000. All excluding VAT. The range depends on scope and on how much of the work is yours to carry.

Who actually does the work?

The people you meet. VeraCruz is a senior studio: strategy and execution sit in the same hands, and nothing is passed to a subcontractor without telling you.

What kinds of projects do you take?

Work where a digital product, a service journey and a physical object have to hold together. That combination is the reason most clients come to us rather than to a specialist in one of the three.

How long does a project take?

A framing engagement takes three to five weeks. An end-to-end project usually runs three to six months, depending on how many decisions sit outside the studio.

Can you work on something confidential or early-stage?

Yes, and most of our work is. We sign your NDA, and we do not publish work without written agreement, which is why parts of the practice are not on this site.

Where are you, and who do you work with?

Brussels. Clients are mostly large organisations in sectors where a mistake is expensive: logistics, energy, medical. We work in English, French and Dutch.

Tell us what you are trying to build.

A few lines about your organisation and what you are working on is enough to start. We reply within two working days.
