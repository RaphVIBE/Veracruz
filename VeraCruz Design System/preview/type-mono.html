MetadataProject 014, speculative, 2025
Code, the only place monospace belongs--font-label and --font-mono

Labels, eyebrows and metadata are set in Outfit at weight 500, sentence case, letter-spacing: 0. Never in a monospace face, never in uppercase, never tracked out past ~0.02em. Monospace plus uppercase plus wide tracking is the house style of AI-generated landing pages; it is the fastest way to make this brand look like one.
